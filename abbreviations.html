02
Professional practice

Engineering & quality

BOQPlural: BOQs
Bill of Quantities

An itemised document describing measured work quantities used for estimating, tendering, pricing, and contract administration.

QA
Quality Assurance

Planned systems and processes intended to prevent defects and ensure work is performed to the required standards.

QC
Quality Control

Inspection, testing, checking, and verification used to identify whether work and materials meet specified requirements.

QA / QC
Quality Assurance / Quality Control

The combined quality-management approach covering preventive processes and verification activities.

03
Client systems

Digital & security

IT
Information Technology

The technical team responsible for approved client-portal access, credentials, and related system assistance.

ID
Identification

Used in “Client ID” for the unique identifier issued to an authorised portal user.

2FA
Two-Factor Authentication

A security method requiring two different forms of verification before access is granted.

IP
Internet Protocol

Used in “IP address,” a network address that may appear in website hosting and security records.

CAPTCHA
Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart

A challenge designed to distinguish a human user from automated activity. reCAPTCHA is the name of Google’s CAPTCHA service referenced in the client-portal interface.

05
Useful distinction

Names—not abbreviations

Jira, reCAPTCHA, WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google Fonts, Everest Bank, Fonepay, and NepalPay are product, organisation, platform, or service names. They are not expanded as AECPS abbreviations. Their appearance identifies a referenced technology or available channel and does not by itself imply partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement.
